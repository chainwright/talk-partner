# Claims

The claims we make about Talk Partner in the README, on screen and in the submission notes, with the status of each on
17 Nov 2026. Rows 1 to 39 stand as they were checked at commit `deebc94`; rows 40 and 41 are the two features added
after it on 17 Nov (the quick-needs board and voice input), and four older rows (3, 4, 22, 36) say what those two
change for them. `EVIDENCE.md` says which runs were made again at the commits of the two features and which were
not. Rows 1, 2, 14, 31 and 34 were changed and rows 43 and 44 added later on 17 Nov, after a second rehearsal run
(commits `4e27d1e` to `30db57a`); they were checked on localhost at `30db57a` and, after the deploy of 17 Nov, 19:05 UTC, on the hosted copy (`devlog/evidence_live/e2e-cloud_live_2026-11-17_4d7edee.txt` 28/28 at 19:06 UTC, `e2e-needs_live_2026-11-17_4d7edee.txt` 12/12). Later that evening the cloud run did not pass there: the organiser of our fifth review ran the same script against the hosted copy at 19:53 and 19:54 UTC and got 17/27 and 26/28 (one cloud card of four; the weekend card took 28.2 s, "who to call" made no card, Remote Config timed out once), and three runs of ours at about 20:20 UTC got 16 or 17 of 27 (rows 13, 28 and 30; the README says what was changed). After the change, at the build of `2c5673e` (deployed 20:55 UTC): `e2e-needs.mjs --url` 12/12, the page loads in Chromium 148 and Chrome 157 with no page error, and `e2e-cloud.mjs --url` 18/27 and 14/27: App Check still refused our machine's browsers, so the cloud step is **not shown working on the hosted copy at the current build** (`devlog/evidence_live/appcheck_probe_2026-11-17_2c5673e.md`). **True** = you can reproduce it from a clone. **True, recorded** = it needs our project
settings or the on-device model, so we give the saved run instead. **Partial** = true with a limit, stated. **Not
claimed** = things we are sometimes assumed to claim and do not. Proof for each row is in `EVIDENCE.md` under the same
number.

Rows 55 to 62 were added on 18 Nov (conversation trees, what the app learns by counting, narrowing that goes broader, colour pictures, the family's own photos, Back and Home, and a new version that arrives). Row 58 is **Partial**. One thing was planned and not built: background removal for photos (row 60).

## Not claimed
- That anyone the simple view is for has tried it (rows 45, 49, 50), or that its wording has been tested with carers.
- That the narrowing step or the on-device "Today in short" gives good results: the first has one recorded run with the cloud model and none on the device, the second none (rows 53, 54).
- That the first-letter helper, the person's own start, the doctor-visit page or "Talk about a photo" help anyone: nobody the app is for has tried them, and no clinician has read the doctor-visit list (rows 77 to 85).
- That the on-device model reads family photos well. It named a mug and a table, and a drawn house, tree and sun; nothing more was measured (row 85).
- That there is AI on a phone's own chip. Gemini Nano through Chrome exists on desktop Chrome only; on a phone the rules make the topic cards and an open question uses online help, with consent.
- That the pictures have been shown to work for adults with aphasia. Since 18 Nov they are our own colour drawings and the family's own photos, which is the direction the research points to (`design/IMAGES_RESEARCH_2026-11-17.md`: small studies), but nobody with aphasia has looked at ours, and a photo keeps its background (rows 59 and 60).
- That Talk Partner helps people with aphasia or their partners. No one living with aphasia has used it. The timed
  trial in `devlog/TRIAL_PROTOCOL.md` has not been run.
- That the diary is a record of what the person ate, drank or felt. It holds what was entered in the app (rows 64, 66).
- That the insight rules or their wording are clinically sound. The thresholds are ours; no nurse, doctor or speech and language therapist has reviewed them (rows 63, 66, 67).
- That a model words the insights or the tip well: neither wording step has run against a real model (row 68).
- That it is a therapy, a diagnosis or a medical device.
- That the on-device cards are good. The recorded runs show the path works and what it returned; the misses on our own set, two
  wrong open cards among them, are listed in `devlog/evidence_r2/heldout_r2_NOTES.md`.
- That the hosted copy (<https://talk-partner.web.app>) shows the on-device step to everyone: that step needs desktop
  Chrome with Gemini Nano downloaded. In such a Chrome it runs on the hosted copy as it does on localhost (recorded on
  17 Nov: `devlog/evidence_live/ondevice_live_2026-11-17_deebc94/RUN_LOG.md`, at the build before the two features
  of rows 40 and 41; at the current build that script was run on localhost only, and on the hosted copy the first
  step was watched once by hand in that Chrome); everyone else is offered the recording
  (row 39). The cloud step works there once cloud help is turned on (`devlog/evidence_live/`); on localhost App Check
  uses a debug token.
- That the classes of mistake the rehearsals found are gone. Each fix is tested on new phrasings (`tests/unit/sim-classes.test.ts`,
  `sim-d-classes.test.ts`, `sim-e-classes.test.ts`, `sim-e2-classes.test.ts`); every rehearsal so far has found classes the one before had not.
  The fixes of 17 Nov (rows 1, 2, 14, 31, 34, 43, 44) have not been run against any rehearsal set yet.
- Any number from the 9–10 Nov prototype (16 of 50, 39 of 50) as a result of this build. They are in the README as the
  reason for one design cut and are marked as pre-period.
- That the person's speech is recognised. It is not, on purpose: recognition of aphasic speech gives confident
  wrong text. The microphone is for the partner's question only.
- That voice input is private or on-device. The recording goes to Gemini in Google's cloud through Firebase AI
  Logic (`ONLY_IN_CLOUD`, `transcribe()` in `web/src/ai/firebaseEngine.ts`), names in it included, after a yes to
  the notice (row 41).
- That the quick-needs board or voice input has been tried by anyone it is for (rows 40 and 41), or that the app
  works offline from a cold start.
- That any AI looks at a family photo, or that a photo's background is removed (row 60).
- That the app's learned lines are more than counts (row 56), or that an AI learns the person.
- Image generation, text-to-speech, sync between two devices, Android or iOS. Every picture is bundled; the picture-generation step that was defined and never called was removed on 17 Nov 2026.
